Troika Handbook

Everything you can do in the Troika music-lessons app, in plain words. Find your role in the menu, or jump to a task. Steps use this style for buttons and menus you tap.

Getting started

Signing in & who can do what

There are three kinds of accounts. What you see depends on your role.

  1. Open the app and sign in with Google or your email.
  2. First time only: pick whether you're a Teacher or a Student. (Coordinators are set up by the office.)
  3. New teacher and student accounts wait for a coordinator to approve them. You'll see an "Account pending approval" screen until then.

Coordinator

Runs the school — students, schedules, money, everything.

Teacher

Sees their own classes, marks attendance, tracks pieces.

Student / Parent

Sees lessons, calendar, payments and invoices.

Finding your way around

  1. The left sidebar is your menu. Your name and role sit at the bottom.
  2. Press ⌘K (Mac) or CtrlK (Windows) anywhere to search — jump to any page, or type a student's name to open them.
  3. Use the sun/moon button (top-right) to switch between light and dark. The app starts in light mode.
Coordinator

Students

Add a student

  1. Go to Students in the sidebar.
  2. Click Add student and fill in name, contact and parent details.
  3. Save. The student now appears in the list and can be enrolled in classes.
💡
Tip: If a student signs up themselves with the same email, their login links automatically to the record you created — no double entry.

Edit, instruments, activate/deactivate

  1. Click a student's name to open their page.
  2. Click Edit to change contact details, parent info, location and notes.
  3. Under Instruments / Subjects, tap the chips to add or remove what they learn. Changes save as you tap.
  4. Use Deactivate to hide a student who has left (nothing is deleted); Reactivate brings them back.
Coordinator

Enrolling a student & multiple classes

Enrol a student in a class

Enrolling sets the fee plan, creates the weekly slot, and generates all the lessons.

  1. Go to Enrolments → New Enrolment (or open a student and click New enrolment).
  2. Pick the student, then the lesson rate for the class. The rate fills in automatically — you can type a different amount for this student if needed.
  3. Choose the payment plan (1, 3 or 10 instalments) and a discount if any (see below).
  4. Pick or create the weekly schedule slot (day, time, teacher, instrument).
  5. Click Save & Generate Lessons. The lessons and payment instalments are created.

Adding several instruments (Guitar + Violin + Voice…)

A student can take as many classes as they like — each is its own enrolment.

  1. After you save the first class, click + Add another class for [name].
  2. The student stays selected — just pick the next instrument's rate and slot, and save again.
  3. Repeat for each class, then click Done. Each class shows as its own card on the student's page (e.g. "Guitar · 2026", "Violin · 2026").

How discounts work

Pick one main discount, and optionally add the multi-lesson one on top.

  • Plan discount — automatic from the plan (1 instalment = 10% off, 3 = 5%, 10 = 0%).
  • Legacy student — 25% off, for long-standing students.
  • Flat ₹ special — a fixed rupee amount you type in.
  • Multi-lesson (+5%) — a tick-box that stacks on top of any of the above.
💡
Discounts apply to tuition only, never to the registration fee.
Coordinator

Schedule & lessons

View and add lessons

  1. Go to Schedule. Switch between Day and Week views, and filter by teacher or instrument.
  2. Click Add lesson to create a one-off lesson (regular, makeup, demo, workshop or special) with its own fee options.
  3. Recurring weekly classes come from enrolments and from Teacher Schedules.

Mark a lesson done, or cancel it

  1. In Day view, click a lesson to expand it.
  2. Click Mark completed when the lesson has happened — this counts it toward the student's lesson total. Reopen undoes it.
  3. Click Cancel this lesson and give a reason. The student is notified automatically.
⚠️
Important: lesson counts, "lessons remaining" and reports only move once lessons are marked completed (by you or the teacher). Get into the habit of ticking them off.
Coordinator

Attendance & charged absences

Mark who attended

  1. Open a student's page and find the lesson in their history.
  2. Mark Attended, or mark an Absence and choose Charged or Not charged.
  3. A charged absence uses up one of the student's paid lessons (and makes them eligible for a makeup); not charged does not.

Find a makeup slot

  1. On the Schedule, expand a lesson and click Find makeup for a student.
  2. The app suggests students at the same location with charged absences, so you can pair up a makeup.
Coordinator

Breaks & rescheduling

Schedule a holiday break

  1. Go to Breaks → New Break.
  2. Set the dates and choose all students or specific ones. Preview shows how many lessons it affects.
  3. Create it — covered lessons are cancelled and marked for reschedule.
  4. Use Auto-Reschedule to have the app find open slots for makeup lessons, or reschedule each one by hand.
💡
On the Schedule, a student who's on a break shows their slot greyed out — a reminder the time is only temporarily free.
Coordinator

Payments & invoices

Record a payment & send the invoice

  1. Go to Payments. Rows are grouped into Overdue, Upcoming and Paid.
  2. When money comes in, click Mark Paid on that instalment. An invoice is generated automatically.
  3. If the invoice ever fails to generate, the payment is still saved as paid and a Generate invoice button appears to retry.
  4. Use the WhatsApp button on a row to open a ready-made reminder message with the amount and invoice link.
Coordinator

Extra (miscellaneous) charges

Add a one-off charge

For recital tickets, books, exam fees and anything outside tuition.

  1. On the Payments page, click Add charge.
  2. Pick the student, type a name (e.g. "Recital tickets"), the amount and a due date.
  3. Save. It appears in Payments like any other item, and gets its own invoice when marked paid.
Coordinator

Lesson rates & locations

Lesson Rates

  1. Go to Lesson Rates.
  2. Pick a teacher, then type the price for each category / location. It saves as you type.
  3. These prices fill in automatically when you enrol a student (and you can still override per student).

Locations

  1. Go to Locations.
  2. Add, rename or remove places (Online, Links, Edvin, a home…).
  3. They then appear in every location dropdown across the app.
Coordinator

Curriculum library

Add teaching resources

  1. Go to Curriculum.
  2. Upload a curriculum spreadsheet to import pieces, exercises and activities in bulk.
  3. Teachers can then search and use these when tracking student progress.
Coordinator

Reports & exporting data

See the numbers & download a spreadsheet

  1. Go to Reports. The cards show revenue collected, lessons delivered, lessons attended and total absences.
  2. The table lists every student with lessons attended, absences, dues collected and outstanding — sorted so the most-active students are on top.
  3. Click Export CSV to download it all (with a totals row) and sort or chart it in Excel / Google Sheets.
Teacher

Your teaching day

Schedule, attendance & completion

  1. Open Schedule (day) or Calendar (month) to see your classes.
  2. For each student, tap Present or Absent.
  3. Tap the tick to mark a lesson Completed. Once everyone is marked, it completes automatically.

Repertoire, notes & materials

  1. Open a lesson and use Pieces to add repertoire and move each piece through Not started → In progress → Completed.
  2. Add a short note for the lesson.
  3. Upload a recording or photo of sheet music to the lesson.
  4. Use Curriculum to search the shared library of pieces and exercises.

Your portal

Lessons, calendar & payments

  1. Home shows your next lesson and a quick summary.
  2. My Lessons lists upcoming and past lessons with attendance; a cancelled lesson shows a "Cancelled" tag.
  3. Calendar shows the month at a glance.
  4. Payments shows what's due and paid. Tap the invoice number to download your invoice.
🔔
The bell (top-right) shows notifications — payment reminders and lesson cancellations.

Common questions

A student's lesson calendar is empty

They probably have no lessons generated yet. Enrol them through the Enrolments page — that creates their schedule and lessons. (Simply adding a student does not.)

"Lessons remaining" or reports show zero

Lessons must be marked completed to count. Ask teachers to tick them off, or do it on the Schedule day view.

A number of lessons per year looks off

The annual lesson count is set automatically from the month a student joins (Jan = 39, tapering to December). It's meant to reduce admin — no need to type it.
